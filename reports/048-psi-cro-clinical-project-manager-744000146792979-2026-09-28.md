## Job Description (archived verbatim)

Clinical Project Manager

Take your career to the next level and lead challenging full-service clinical research projects in phase II and III, managing the full scope of responsibilities and getting hands-on involvement in every aspect of the study.

Responsibilities include managing small projects under direct or general supervision with primary responsibility for deliverables on time and on budget; performing a regional lead or project co-manager role in complex international projects; leading project teams; training, mentoring and coaching CRAs, Project Coordination Associates and Site Management Associates; participating in feasibility research; and supporting proposals, client presentations and bid-defense meetings.

Qualifications:

- Bachelor's degree or equivalent combination of education, training and experience; equivalency defined as minimum five years in pharmaceutical/healthcare industry and clinical research.
- At least five-year industry experience in clinical research.
- At least two-year recent clinical research experience as project manager/equivalent (e.g., CTM, Clinical Study Team Lead).
- Regional clinical project-management experience, including management of all project services from startup to closure.
- Therapeutic experience in Oncology, Gastroenterology, and/or Radiopharmaceuticals is a plus.
- Excellent written and verbal communication and ability to build stakeholder relationships.
